## Voice and accessibility

Everything written here, across the skills, the templates, the README, and in
chat, follows the house voice. The canonical statement lives in
`plugins/product-steward/skills/steward-the-product/SKILL.md` under "Voice and
access", so read it before you write. The rules in brief:

- Calm science-and-reason tone, complete sentences, the Oxford comma, and no em dashes.
- Lowercase "webs" and lowercase UI chrome. Proper nouns in explanatory prose keep their natural casing.
- No AI-slop vocabulary and no stock example names. "Sarah Chen" is off the table.
- No self-certifying virtue words. Strike "honest," "honestly," "the truth is," and "let me be straight".
- No ableist or sensory-ability metaphors.
- Skip the false contrast. State the positive directly, ahead of any trailing ", not X" clause or leading "not X, but Y" clause.
- Open a sentence with its subject or a plain adverb, keeping the coordinating conjunctions (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) inside the sentence where they join clauses.
- Accessibility is a product question the artifacts carry from the first draft: cognitive load, plain language, and inclusive design belong in them from the start.

These rules are this repo's own. They stand on their own, without pointing at any
file outside this repository.

## Push back

When a request, a plan, or a draft has a real problem, say so and propose the
better path. Agreeing to be agreeable wastes webs's time. Push back with reasons,
and defer once she has decided.
